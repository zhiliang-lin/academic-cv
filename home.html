Biography

🎉Hi, my name is Zhiliang Lin (林志良🔈 listen). I am a quantitative researcher interested in health behaviour, mental health and digital media. I use longitudinal data and computational methods to study how everyday behaviours such as physical activity, sleep, diet and media use relate to body weight and wellbeing over time.

I am currently completing a Subsequent Master of Science in Statistical Data Analysis at Ghent University, where my thesis examines the bidirectional relationship between body mass index and depression in a Belgian cohort. I have six years of work experience, including working as a Teaching Associate at Guangzhou Nanfang College and a Research Associate at Shenzhen University. I have a B.A. in Journalism from the Southwestern University of Finance and Economics and an M.A. in Communication from Jinan University.

Outside of academia, I enjoy reading, running, gardening, and football. And I'm a big fan of the Chinese classic novel The Story of the Stone and the football club Bayern Munich.

Research Projects

  • M.Sc. thesis: Bidirectional associations between body mass index and depression in the Belgian BELHEALTH cohort (data provided by Sciensano). Five survey waves over 66 months; linear mixed models, latent class growth analysis and spline models on 12,803 adults with repeated measures. The association runs in both directions but is stronger from BMI to depression, is U-shaped, and stayed stable across the pandemic-to-post-pandemic transition.
  • Zhiliang Lin. From Health Consciousness to Connected Action: Online Information Seeking and Social Support as Serial Mediators of Healthy Behaviors among Chinese College Students. Extended abstract accepted at the IAMCR 2026 Annual Conference (not presented); revised full paper in preparation.
